## Channel distinction (important)

The comment and the § 553(e) petition are procedurally distinct. A PRA comment carries no
entitlement to the reasoned, separately reviewable disposition that 5 U.S.C. §§ 553(e) and 555(e)
and 24 C.F.R. § 10.20 provide. The comment, filed July 6, 2026, adds to the PRA record; the petition converts
the relevant record into a proceeding HUD must conclude with a reasoned response. The comment's aggregate,
entity-level design reflects Form HUD-27061's public, FOIA-releasable posture; the companion rule
the petition seeks is broader because a privacy-tiered architecture can carry regulator-only fields
under a Privacy Act system of records. The comment identifies the minimum defect in the pending
collection; the petition seeks reasoned consideration of a durable, cross-program architecture.
(This is the reconciler the Note states at footnote 153.)
