## What the comment is, and is not

The comment, filed July 6, 2026, is part of the public administrative record for this collection. Its central
contention — that the collection is "described broader than built" — is the same contention the Note's Part I.E
develops from primary Federal Register and OIRA sources. The comment is not the Note's vehicle (the § 553(e)
petition is), not the Note's thesis, and not a standing predicate; the Note cites it only as part of the public
PRA record, never as authority. It was submitted by the author in his individual capacity, with no institutional
affiliation.
