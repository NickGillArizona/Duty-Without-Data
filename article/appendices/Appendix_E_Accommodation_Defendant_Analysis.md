### E.1.1 Claim-Specificity Validation (cited by fn 86)

No claim-specificity outcome analysis is reported in the Note or in this appendix. The
grounds, as fn 86 discloses: the claim-specificity outcome cells are sparse (the adjusted
model fails from complete separation), and the field failed blind validation — 66.6%
agreement on determinate rows (271/407) between the recorded key and a blind re-read,
rising to 74.7% (304/407) under majority-vote adjudication with an independent third
read — below the 85% bar the merits-gate driver fields cleared. These figures are
reproducibility measures for the coding procedure, not accuracy; they are reported so a
reader can see exactly why the field was not used.

The validation record:
