## Note on appendix lettering

The suite's letters are nonsequential (A, A-4, A-6, A-7, B, C, D, E, H, K, L, M, P, T, Y) because they match the Note's own citations, which are stable identifiers. Readers should not infer that unlisted letters or numbers are missing appendices: they do not exist in this archive. Unlisted letters belong to uncited exploratory analyses retained in the project's private research records (see `replication/DATA_PROVENANCE.md`).
