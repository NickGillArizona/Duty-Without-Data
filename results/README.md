# What is in `results/` — and what is citable

This directory holds three tiers of material, and this page draws the boundaries so readers do
not cite the wrong tier: **current citable** artifacts (section 1 — the canonical machine
outputs the ledger routes to), **supporting** artifacts and supplementary memos (section 2 —
registered study data and background memoranda the Note does not rely on for printed numbers),
and **as-run validation records** (section 3 — frozen run directories kept byte-stable as
evidence of what was done). The quick test for any file: if a number in the Note rests on it,
[`../article/CLAIMS_LEDGER.csv`](../article/CLAIMS_LEDGER.csv) names the file and the script
that produced it. Research memos and their companion data live in
[`supporting/`](supporting/); the memos that remain at this level are listed in section 2.

## 1. Current citable — canonical machine outputs, routed through the ledger

These regenerate from the raw database with the commands in [`../replication/REPRODUCE.md`](../replication/REPRODUCE.md),
using the scripts in [`../scripts/`](../scripts/), and they are the artifacts the claims ledger
points at.

- Core statistics: `unified_stats.json`, `unified_stats_t2_narrow.json`, and their script-generated
  reports `unified_stats_report.md` and `unified_stats_t2_narrow_report.md`
  (`scripts/recompute_stats_unified.py`).
- Headline analyses: `bootstrap_ci_results.json`, `decomposition_results.json`,
  `h1_h2_results.json`, `h5_results.json`, `h6_results.json`, `h7_results.json`, `h8_results.json`,
  `robustness_checks_output.txt`, `extended_crosstabs_results.json`,
  `pro_se_mechanism_divergence_results.json`,
  `supplemental_classification_results.json`, and
  [`supporting/screened_pleading_loss_missing_cases_audit.json`](supporting/screened_pleading_loss_missing_cases_audit.json).
- Appendix recomputation: `appendix_data.json` and the script-generated `appendix_report.md`
  (`scripts/recompute_all_appendices.py`); `circuit_district_deep_dive_results.json` and the
  script-generated `circuit_district_deep_dive_analysis.md` (`scripts/circuit_district_deep_dive.py`).
- Program and stock data: `posh_results.json`, `reac_results.json`, `cdbg_results.json`,
  `housing_stock_results.json`, `ahs_2023_accessibility_results.json`,
  `lihtc_accessibility_audit_results.json`, `nspire_ufas_crosswalk.json`,
  `qap_accessibility_2025_2026.json`.
- Census/PUMS replication: `pums_se_results.json`, `pums_sensitivity_results.json`, and
  [`supporting/pums_results.csv`](supporting/pums_results.csv).
- Figures: [`figures/`](figures/) — the README's light/dark SVG pair, generated by
  `scripts/make_fig1.py` directly from the canonical JSON artifacts.

## 2. Supporting — registered study artifacts and supplementary memos

The exploratory LLM-assisted research memoranda that formerly accompanied this layer are
retained in the project's private research records, not published here (see
[`../replication/DATA_PROVENANCE.md`](../replication/DATA_PROVENANCE.md)); their negative
and superseded results are summarized in
[`../article/EVIDENCE_AND_LIMITS.md`](../article/EVIDENCE_AND_LIMITS.md), the canonical
statement of the evidence boundary and the superseded document-level tiers.

Three supplementary memos remain in this directory because printed or archived pointers
name them: `comparators_state_and_international.md`,
`hud_existing_disability_data_systems.md`, and `post_alliance_standing_audit.md` — plus
the archived perimeter census at
[`supporting/perimeter_census.md`](supporting/perimeter_census.md), which the
manuscript's repository pointer for the perimeter census names. Their status: not
peer-reviewed, not final, and not endorsed as publication-ready claims; verify the
relevant propositions against primary sources before relying on them outside exploratory
or background use. [`supporting/`](supporting/) otherwise holds registered study
artifacts (CSV/JSON/TXT data files), not memoranda.

Companion artifacts generated for these clusters (for example
`consent_decree_inventory_integration.json`, `pre1991_statutory_gap_analysis.json`,
`state_complaint_panel_results.json` and the two `state_complaint_panel_*.svg` figures here, and
`recap_discrepant_cases_detail.json` in `supporting/`) share the supplementary status.

One nuance: the manuscript's footnote 147 cites two of these memos by filename —
`hud_existing_disability_data_systems.md` and `comparators_state_and_international.md` — as archived
inventories of HUD's existing data fragments. That citation pins their filenames (do not move or
rename them) and identifies them as part of the archive; it does not upgrade their verification
posture, which remains the supplementary posture stated above.

Source URLs, retrieval dates, and redistribution scope for all external inputs are in
[`../replication/DATA_PROVENANCE.md`](../replication/DATA_PROVENANCE.md).

## 3. As-run validation records — the run directories

Two subdirectories here are frozen records of validation and audit runs. Their contents are
kept byte-stable as evidence of what was done, and their internal wording is the as-run
vocabulary of the run they document — do not normalize it. Historical and superseded
outcome material (the document-level series) is labeled where it appears; the canonical
statement of what was superseded is
[`../article/EVIDENCE_AND_LIMITS.md`](../article/EVIDENCE_AND_LIMITS.md).

| Directory | What it records | Where it is summarized |
|---|---|---|
| [`counsel_timing_audit/`](counsel_timing_audit/) | The docket-level counsel-timing audit over the eighteen qualifying cases (cited by the manuscript; also current citable through its README) | [`counsel_timing_audit/README.md`](counsel_timing_audit/README.md) |
| [`specificity_threelayer/`](specificity_threelayer/) | The three-layer claim-specificity comparison behind the failed-validation disclosure | [`specificity_threelayer/THREELAYER_SUMMARY.md`](specificity_threelayer/THREELAYER_SUMMARY.md); [Appendix E § E.1](../article/appendices/Appendix_E_Accommodation_Defendant_Analysis.md) |

A third as-run record of the same kind is published outside this directory: the
preregistered comparator verification run of July 2026, including the
`recoding_2026-07-07/` re-coding record, is at
[`../replication/comparator/`](../replication/comparator/) and is summarized in
[`../method/comparator_validation.md`](../method/comparator_validation.md) and
[Appendix A-6](../article/appendices/Appendix_A6_Comparator_Analysis.md).
