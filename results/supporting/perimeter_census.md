The census was restated to 594 units (via 598) by merging eight cross-caption same-action appellate units (see `../../ERRATA.md`); the perimeter re-classification on the 594 basis is queued and the class counts below are unchanged registered figures.

---

The census classifies program nexus for the 606 decided case-level units. Of the 606
units, 601 joined the SOL-12 program-nexus records (5 unjoined) and 533 yielded resolvable
opinion text (73 did not). Two independent seats classified all 533 (raw agreement 89.5
percent); after adjudication and a human review gate (144 unit-level reviews; one
reclassification), 100 units sit inside the direct regulatory perimeter (79
PHA-administered transactions; 21 other HUD-assisted recipients), 359 outside it (36
HUD-connected outside the direct perimeter; 323 private-market), and 74 coded units could
not be classified (65 with insufficient records; 9 presenting no housing transaction).
606 = 100 + 359 + 74 + 73.

Disclosure 1 (PRIVATE_MARKET residual, mandatory): the private-market cell is a not-HUD
residual; it records only that the opinion states no HUD program connection, includes
municipal, state, USDA-assisted, and Treasury-emergency-assistance arrangements alongside
unassisted private housing, and is not a finding that any unit was unsubsidized.

Disclosure 2 (UNAVAILABLE candor, mandatory): no gap in these figures is an absence
claim -- the 73 no-text units exist in the census but their opinions were not resolvable
on disk (including 2 unresolved file-path defects); the 65 record-insufficient units have
opinions that do not permit program classification under the frozen categories; the 9
no-transaction units present no housing transaction to classify.
