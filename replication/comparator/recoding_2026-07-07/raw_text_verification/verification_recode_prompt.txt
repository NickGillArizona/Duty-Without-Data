=== FAMILY CODES (class-neutral rubric) ===

A - INSTITUTIONALLY-HELD-FACT DEFICIT. The pivotal missing or insufficiently pleaded allegation is a fact the defendant institution (landlord, property manager, housing authority, lender, association) would ordinarily record or control: whether a request was made and what the response was; dates and timing of requests, decisions, or notices; internal decisions, policies, file status, or the responsible actor; unit, building, or premises facts; whether and when the defendant knew or was notified; whether and how a denial occurred.

B - PLAINTIFF-KNOWN-FACT DEFICIT. The pivotal missing allegation is primarily a fact within the plaintiff's own ordinary knowledge: what happened to them, who said or did what to them, where and when an incident occurred, their own circumstances or qualifications, or facts supporting motive or differential treatment that do not depend on institutional records (for example: purely conclusory allegations of animus; no facts of the plaintiff's own experience; no similarly situated person identified from the plaintiff's own observation).

C - LEGAL INSUFFICIENCY INDEPENDENT OF FACT DETAIL. The defect would remain even with complete factual detail: standing, subject-matter jurisdiction, statute of limitations, immunity, claim preclusion or exhaustion, wrong or improper defendant, property or conduct not covered by the statute, or no cognizable legal theory.

UNCLEAR - the opinion does not give enough information to classify the defect.

MISFILTER - the opinion on its face is not a pleading-stage defense win (the claim survived, the plaintiff prevailed, or the disposition rests on summary-judgment evidence or merits-stage findings).
