| Sept. 13, 1988 | Congress enacts the Fair Housing Amendments Act, adding 42 U.S.C. § 3614a (express authority to issue "rules for the collection, maintenance, and analysis of appropriate data") and § 3608(e)(6) (annual reporting on enumerated characteristics, "handicap" among them), in the same statute as the § 3604(f) disability duties. | Pub. | 2003–2019 | Form HUD-27061 is approved and periodically renewed as a race-and-ethnicity collection across nine ICR cycles. | Per-cycle reginfo.gov records archived as `icr_<YYYYMM>-2535-001.html` / `doc_<YYYYMM>-2535-001.html`; supporting statements `SSA_2006`, `SSA_2014`, `SSA_2019` |
| Sept. 27, 2022 | HUD's 60-day notice proposes updating the form "to collect protected class data as required by the Fair Housing Act and HUD regulations at 24 CFR 121." | 87 Fed. Reg. 58,524; archived: [`87FR58524_proposal.pdf`](./87FR58524_proposal.pdf) |
| 2022 comment period | Public comments include the Jones Day / SAGE submission invoking Part 121 and the Williams Institute submission. | Archived: [`comment_SAGE.pdf`](./comment_SAGE.pdf), [`comment_Williams1.pdf`](./comment_Williams1.pdf) |

Reg. 5,370; archived: [`88FR5370_30day_notice.pdf`](./88FR5370_30day_notice.pdf) |

**Record discipline.** This chronology states the located public record through the
archived status pulls' dates. It asserts neither that the collection has been renewed nor
that it has lapsed; either outcome remains open, and the argument the Note builds on this
record holds on both branches.
