## 2. Background

HUD's Form HUD-27061 ("Race and Ethnic Data Collection") has been continuously approved by OIRA under OMB Control No. 2535-0113 since 2003. The publicly available OIRA record for the 2003 cycles (reference numbers 200302-2535-001 and 200308-2535-001) contains no attached documents; the initial Supporting Statements and Federal Register submission packages are therefore not accessible through RegInfo.gov. These records are needed to complete a longitudinal scholarly audit of HUD's administration of OMB Control No. 2535-0113 from the initial collection in 2003 through the 2023 renewal.

## 3. Fee Waiver Request

Pursuant to 5 U.S.C. § 552(a)(4)(A)(iii) and 24 CFR § 15.106, I request a waiver of all applicable fees on the following grounds:

**(a)** The subject matter concerns "the operations or activities of the government" — specifically, HUD's initial adoption of a standardized civil rights data collection form and the agency's justifications to OIRA under the Paperwork Reduction Act for that adoption.

**(b)** The disclosure is "likely to contribute" to public understanding of those operations — the records would illuminate how HUD structured, justified, and scoped Form HUD-27061 at its inception, information which is material to understanding the evolution of the collection across subsequent renewal cycles.

**(c)** The disclosure will contribute to the understanding of a reasonably broad audience — the research is being prepared for publication in a law review and is of interest to administrative-law scholars, fair-housing advocates, disability-rights organizations, and federal agencies that administer analogous civil rights data collections.

**(d)** The disclosure is not primarily in my commercial interest — I am a JD candidate conducting non-commercial scholarly research for publication. I have no commercial stake in the records requested.
